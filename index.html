ScopeKeeper Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information the ScopeKeeper Android app ("ScopeKeeper", "the app", "we") collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. ScopeKeeper helps freelancers and businesses record project scope, change requests and client approvals.

Information you provide

  • Account: your email address, name and password (the password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us).
  • Business details: business name, phone number, default currency and similar profile details you choose to enter.
  • Your work data: clients (name, optional email, phone and notes), projects, scope versions, change requests, costs and activity history that you create.

Client approvals

When you send an approval link, the terms you send and your client's name are stored in an approval record. When your client opens the link in a web browser and makes a decision, their decision, the name they type, any comment and the server time are stored so both of you have a record. Approval links are long random codes, expire, and can be revoked by you at any time. Clients do not need an account.

Information collected automatically

  • Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and diagnostic data such as device model, operating system version, app version, and an identifier for the installation and your account. It does not include the content of your projects or clients.
  • Advertising: the free version of ScopeKeeper shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and information about ad interactions, in order to show, measure and limit ads. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice from the app's Settings.

How we use information

  • To provide the app: sign-in, storing and syncing your data across sessions, and recording client approvals.
  • To fix crashes and improve reliability.
  • To show ads in the free version.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your work data or your clients' data for advertising.

Service providers

We use Google services to run the app: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore (data storage, United States region), Firebase Crashlytics and Google AdMob. Their handling of data is described in Firebase's privacy information and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored by Google Cloud with encryption at rest. Access to your data is restricted to your signed-in account by security rules; approval records can be read only by someone holding the secret link.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete account; this permanently removes your account and all of your projects, clients, change requests, activity and approval records. You can also ask us to delete your account and data by emailing the address below. Crash and advertising data held by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies.

Children

ScopeKeeper is a business tool intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can edit or delete most information directly in the app, or contact us to exercise these rights.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be noted in the app or on this page.
